## 3. THE PLATFORMS

### 3.1 ⭐ WHATSAPP — your single most valuable channel

For an India-based artist this is not a minor platform. It is *the* platform, and it's the one most likely to be skipped because it doesn't feel like marketing.

**Three separate tools:**

**① The personal message (T-7).** To your top ~40 people. **Individually. Never as a broadcast.**
```
hey — so I actually finished it. my song "[SONG]" is out [DATE].

if you've got 3 minutes on friday, the thing that genuinely helps most is:
1. play it once all the way through (don't skip — it counts differently)
2. hit save/like on spotify
3. add it to any one of your own playlists

that's it, and it makes a stupidly large difference to how the algorithm treats it.

[LINK]

thank you 🖤
```
A broadcast is for updates. A personal message is for the ask. **Never send both to the same person in the same week.**

**② WhatsApp Status.** Post to it daily through release week, exactly like an Instagram story. Your Status audience is your *actual* contacts — people far more likely to act than a follower. Most artists never use it. Use the same three-frame system from doc 1.

**③ Group chats.** College groups, hostel groups, society groups, family groups. Post **once** in each, at T, with the link and a sentence. Not twice. These are relationships, not channels, and overposting costs you more than the streams are worth.

**The gap rule:** one personal ask at T-7, one at T+30 if there's a real milestone. That's two asks in the whole campaign. Any more and you burn goodwill you'll need for release #2.

### 3.2 ⭐ THE EMAIL LIST

**Email converts ~10× better than social.** The catch is that it pays off on your *next* release, not this one — which is exactly why it feels skippable and exactly why you shouldn't skip it.

- [ ] **Mailchimp free tier** or **Buttondown** — set up at T-30
- [ ] Signup form on the smart link, on your website, and as a story link once a fortnight
- [ ] Offer something: "the demo, the lyric sheet and the first thing I ever wrote about this song" as a welcome email
- [ ] **Send 4 emails in the whole window:** announcement (T-30), release day (T), one-month wrap (T+28), acoustic (T+42)
- [ ] Write them like letters, not newsletters. No headers, no images, no "hey fam". Just you, writing.
- [ ] **Target: 100 subscribers by T+55.** That's a modest number and it's worth more than 1,000 Instagram followers.

### 3.3 AMAZON MUSIC FOR ARTISTS — the pitch nobody uses

Amazon has a **New Release Pitch** tool with far less competition than Spotify's, and — crucially — **the window extends to 14 days *after* release**, unlike Spotify's, which closes at release. Almost no independent artist submits it.

- [ ] Claim the profile at `artists.amazonmusic.com`
- [ ] Submit the New Release Pitch **pre-release, or by T+14 at the latest**
- Fields: up to 3 genres, 3 moods, 3 similar artists, **audience location** (name your diaspora markets explicitly), and marketing drivers
- Use the Amazon pitch text from the master plan's G2
- [ ] Upload artist image + banner

**Twenty minutes, genuinely decent odds.** Do it.

### 3.5 ⭐ REDDIT — the highest ceiling, and the fastest way to get banned

Reddit can put a first single in front of thousands of genuinely interested people. It can also permanently ban a new account for a single self-promotional post, and it is *extremely* good at detecting an account created to promote something.

**The protocol. It is not optional.**

> **Join at T-45. Comment — never post — for four full weeks. Build real karma in non-music subreddits too. Post your own track once, at T or T+1, in the subreddits that explicitly allow it.**

**The subreddits:**

| Subreddit | Rules | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **r/IndieMusicFeedback** | Feedback-for-feedback | ⭐ **Start here.** Explicitly built for this. Give 3 real critiques, get one back. |
| **r/WeAreTheMusicMakers** | No self-promo in posts | Participate in the craft threads; the weekly feedback thread is where you post |
| **r/listentothis** | Very strict formatting | High reward if it lands, instant removal if the format's wrong. **Read the rules twice.** |
| **r/indianmusic** | Moderate | ⭐ Your home market, small and engaged |
| **r/india** / **r/[yourcity]** | Strict on self-promo | Only with a genuine angle, and only after real participation |
| **r/bedroompop**, **r/indieheads**, genre subs | Varies | Check each sub's rules individually |
| **r/edmproduction**, **r/musicproduction** | No self-promo | The *process* posts from doc 1 Family G work here |
| **r/SpotifyPlaylistShare** | Permissive | Low quality, but free |

**What actually works on Reddit:** not "here's my song". A post about *how you did something* — the mixing problem you solved, the tax paperwork, releasing from India — that happens to have your song in it. Reddit rewards usefulness and punishes promotion, and the distinction is genuinely about which one the post is.

### 3.6 GENIUS — 30 minutes, permanent

- [ ] Create the artist page and add the full lyrics for `[SONG]`
- [ ] **Annotate 3–5 lines yourself** — Genius lets verified artists annotate, and these show up in searches
- [ ] Add credits: producer, mixer, writer, release date

**Why:** Genius lyrics feed Google's lyric box and, in some cases, DSP lyric displays. When someone half-remembers your line and googles it, this is what catches them. Permanent, free, one-time.

### 3.7 SOUNDCLOUD

Two real uses, one imagined one.

**Real use 1 — private pre-release links for curators.** A SoundCloud private link is the standard way to send an unreleased track to a curator or blogger. You need this from T-21. It's the main reason to have the account.

**Real use 2 — the Repost network.** Free tier gets you into repost chains with other small artists. Modest, but free.

**The imagined use:** SoundCloud as a streaming platform where you'll build an audience. It isn't, not for you, not in 2026. Set it up, use it as a delivery mechanism, don't invest in it.

- [ ] Claim `[HANDLE]`, upload the track (public at T)
- [ ] Private link ready by T-21

### 3.8 JIOSAAVN / WYNK / GAANA — non-negotiable for the India phase

DistroKid delivers to all of these. **What it doesn't do is claim your artist profile.**

- [ ] Opt in via the distributor at upload (T-38)
- [ ] **Claim the JioSaavn artist profile** — `jiosaavn.com/artists`
- [ ] Verify the track appears on each at T+1
- [ ] Add profile images and bio where the platform allows

These matter specifically because your Phase 5 (T+7 → T+27) is domestic, and a meaningful share of Indian listening happens off Spotify. Twenty minutes each.

### 3.9 BANDCAMP — small, but the best per-fan economics anywhere

- [ ] Set up the page at T-21
- [ ] Sell the WAV + the instrumental, name-your-price with a minimum
- [ ] Release-day post + link it once from stories
- [ ] **Bandcamp Fridays** — on those days the platform waives its revenue share. If one falls in your window, make it a small event.

You will not make much money here. But the people who *do* buy are your actual fans, and knowing who they are is worth more than the money.

### 3.10 LINKEDIN — genuinely, unexpectedly worth ten minutes

Engagement on a personal milestone post on LinkedIn in India is disproportionate, and the audience is different from every other platform — professionals, alumni, people in adjacent industries.

- [ ] **One post at T.** Frame it as what it is: an engineering student who wrote, produced, mixed and released a single independently while finishing a degree. That's a story LinkedIn's algorithm and audience both like.
- [ ] **One post at T+55** — the retrospective, framed as "what building and shipping a creative project alone taught me". This one travels.
- [ ] Useful beyond ego: **sync licensing, brand partnerships, and Indian music-industry contacts** genuinely live here.

Two posts in the entire campaign. Don't do more.

### 3.11 PINTEREST — the longest half-life on the internet

A Pinterest pin can drive traffic for **two years**. Nothing else in this plan does that.

- [ ] Business account, 20 minutes at T-21
- [ ] Pin: lyric graphics, cover art, aesthetic boards matched to the song's mood, the vertical key art
- [ ] **Every pin links to `[LINK]`**
- [ ] Boards: "lyrics", "[SONG]", "songwriting", "home studio"
- [ ] Pin 2–3 a week; it takes five minutes

Best fit for the lyric cards and the designed story graphics you're already making for Instagram — this is the one place where reposting an Instagram asset verbatim is *correct*, because the audiences don't overlap at all.

### 3.13 HYGIENE — 30 minutes, once, never again

- [ ] **Shazam** — verify your track resolves correctly at T+1. It auto-populates; you're just checking.
- [ ] **MusicBrainz** — add yourself and the release. Feeds a surprising number of downstream services.
- [ ] **Discogs** — add the release. Credibility, collectors, and it shows up in searches.
- [ ] **Namechk / KnowEm** — confirm you've claimed the handle everywhere, including platforms you'll never use.

### 3.14 DISCORD — not yet, and here's the threshold

**An empty Discord server reads worse than no Discord server.** A visitor who joins and finds three people and no messages learns something bad about you.

**The threshold:** build it when you have **~50 people who have taken an action** — replied to a story, joined the broadcast channel, emailed you, bought something on Bandcamp. Not 50 followers. 50 people who did something.

Realistically that's release #2 territory. Until then, the **Instagram Broadcast Channel** (doc 1, §3.5) does the same job with zero setup and no empty-room problem.

---

### 3.15 TWITCH — skip this release

Live streaming to zero viewers is demoralising in a way that will cost you more in morale than it returns in anything. The format also needs consistency — twice a week, same time, for months — which is directly at odds with a 100-day campaign where your capacity is already committed.

**Revisit at release #3, if you want to.** Not now.

## 4. THE DO-NOT-BOTHER LIST

Things that will be suggested to you, or that you'll be tempted by. Each one is a real way to waste money or damage the release.

| ❌ | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Paid guaranteed playlist placement** | Payola. Spotify removes tracks and can penalise your profile. Bot streams poison your listener data permanently. |
| **Buying followers or streams** | Same, worse. The algorithm reads low-completion plays from dead accounts as *the song is bad*. |
| **Playlist Push and $300+ campaign services** | Wrong tier. That money is 1.5× your entire ad budget. |
| **Jango / paid radio airplay** | It's an ad buy dressed as radio. Treat the cost as ads and spend it on Meta instead. |
| **DMs offering "music promotion"** | Every single one is a scam or a bot farm. Without exception. |
| **A Discord server before ~50 real fans** | An empty room. |
| **Twitch at zero followers** | Morale cost exceeds any return. |
| **Web3 / NFT music platforms** | Not where your listeners are. |
| **Posting identical content to every platform** | The exact thing you said you wanted to avoid — and it's also the thing the algorithms punish (watermarks, duplicate detection). |
| **Chasing a specific viral audio trend** | By the time you've made it, it's over. Chase *shapes*, not sounds. |
| **A second distributor "for wider reach"** | Duplicate delivery, split royalties, takedowns. One distributor, always. |
| **IPRS *and* BMI** | You can only be in one PRO worldwide. See master plan C2. |
| **DistroKid Publishing *and* Songtrust** | They'll fight over the same registrations and freeze your payouts. |
| **Spotify Marquee this release** | You're not eligible. Needs 5,000+ monthly listeners in-market. |
| **Re-uploading the same recording with a new ISRC** | Splits stream counts; looks like fraud. |
| **Buying a "verified" badge anywhere as a growth strategy** | It changes nothing about reach. |
