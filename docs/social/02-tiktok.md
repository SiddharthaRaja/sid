## 0. READ THIS FIRST — THE INDIA PROBLEM ⚠️

**TikTok has been blocked in India since June 2020 under Section 69A of the IT Act, and the ban still stands.** In August 2025 the site briefly loaded without a VPN and everyone got excited; TikTok itself confirmed it was an ISP misconfiguration and that it "continues to remain inaccessible in India." Nothing has changed since.

Three consequences you have to plan around, and none of them are optional:

**1. TikTok contributes nothing to your India phase.** The master plan's Phase 5 (T+7 → T+27, domestic focus) has *zero* TikTok component. Your friends, your college, your local scene — none of them are there. Don't count a single Indian listener from this platform.

**2. TikTok is your diaspora and Western platform, exclusively.** Which is fine — that's where the Indian diaspora in the US, UK, Canada, UAE and Australia actually is, and TikTok is still the single best cold-discovery surface for music in those markets. It's the right tool, pointed at the right phase. It's just pointed at phase 6, not phase 5.

**3. You need a VPN to operate it, and that has practical costs.**

| Friction | Reality | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Uploading needs a VPN session every time | Slow, occasionally drops mid-upload | Upload on the **web**, not mobile — far more stable over a VPN |
| Account region is set at signup | Sets your initial content pool | **This is an advantage — create the account on a US or UK VPN.** You *want* to be seeded to Western viewers. |
| Region mismatch (India IP + US account) can trip checks | Rare but real; can cause temporary limits | Use one consistent VPN region. Never switch countries between sessions. |
| Live streaming | Effectively off the table | Skip it. Not a loss at your stage. |
| Monetisation programmes | You're not eligible anyway | Irrelevant for a first release |

**The best mitigation, and you should do this:** use **TikTok's own web scheduler** (schedule up to 10 days ahead from the desktop upload page) or a third-party scheduler with a TikTok integration (Metricool, Later, Buffer — all in the link directory). Batch-schedule 10 days of posts in one VPN session, once a week. That turns a daily friction into a weekly one, and it also solves the timezone problem in §10 — your audience's prime time is the middle of your night.

**The honest ROI verdict:** TikTok is a **second-priority platform for you from T-45 to T+20, and a first-priority platform from T+21 to T+55.** Set it up now and run a low-density mirror so the account has history and the algorithm has data — then turn the density up exactly when your campaign turns toward the diaspora. Starting a TikTok account cold at T+28 wastes the first two weeks teaching the algorithm who you are.

## 1. THE THESIS — WHY TIKTOK IS NOT A REELS MIRROR

You will post the same *files* to TikTok, Reels and Shorts. That's correct and you should keep doing it.

But **the same file with the same caption and the same hook is three copies of one post. The same file with three different framings is three posts.** The difference costs you ninety seconds per asset and it roughly doubles what the campaign is worth.

What makes TikTok structurally different:

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| **Who sees it** | Biased toward people who already follow you | Biased toward strangers. Follower count barely matters. |
| **What ranks** | Sends per reach, watch time, relationships | Watch time and rewatches, above everything |
| **Search** | Weak, improving | **A real search engine.** People use TikTok like Google. This is the biggest strategic difference. |
| **Series** | Mildly rewarded | **Heavily rewarded.** Part 1 / Part 2 formats compound. |
| **Comments** | A nice-to-have | **A content surface.** Replying with a video is a first-class post type. |
| **Sound** | The track is a nice extra | **The track is the product.** Sound adoption is the whole game. |
| **Tolerance for repetition** | Low — followers get tired | **High** — every post meets a new pool |
| **Lifespan of a post** | ~48 hours | **Weeks.** Old posts resurface constantly. |
| **Register** | Considered, designed | Faster, rougher, more direct. Polish reads as an ad. |

**The one-sentence version:** Instagram is a *profile* with videos on it. TikTok is a *feed* your videos enter. On Instagram you're building a place people visit. On TikTok you're buying lottery tickets with content, and the price of a ticket is one upload.

## 3. THE SOUND — THE ONLY TIKTOK CONCEPT THAT REALLY MATTERS

On every other platform, you post content and the song is in it. On TikTok, **the song is a separate object that lives in a library, and your goal is to get it into other people's hands.** A video with 200k views that used a *different* sound did nothing for you. A video with 4k views that put your sound in front of 40 creators is worth more.

**How sound adoption actually happens, in order of likelihood:**

1. **You use it yourself, a lot.** Every post you make with the official sound adds to that sound's page. A sound page with 60 videos looks alive; a sound page with 3 looks dead, and creators check.
2. **You ask, specifically and easily.** "The sound's up, use it" is too vague. "The sound's up — the 8 seconds from 0:47 is the bit that works over anything" is a usable instruction.
3. **You give people a reason.** A sound that works over a specific *kind* of video (a walk, a getting-ready, a photo dump, a goodbye) gets used. A sound that's just a good song doesn't.
4. **Duet bait.** You leave a gap. Someone fills it.
5. **The street team.** 15 people posting one story each, using the sound, on release day.

**The three practical asks to make, in order:**
- T-14: "the pre-save's up" (no sound yet)
- T: "the sound is live — search `[ARTIST] [SONG]`" + a video showing exactly where to find it
- T+3: "here's the 8 seconds of it that works over anything" — and then post three different videos using exactly those 8 seconds, so there's a template to copy

**⚠️ Name the sound correctly.** If you post a clip *before* release, TikTok labels it "original sound — [HANDLE]". After release, use the **official track** from the library instead, so the plays consolidate onto one sound page. Two sound pages for one song is a real and permanent loss.

## 4. THE RULES OF A TIKTOK MUSIC POST

1. **Hook in the first 1 second, not 1.5.** TikTok's scroll is faster than Instagram's. The first frame must already be doing something.
2. **On-screen text in frame one, always.** It's the stop-scroll mechanism and it's OCR'd for search.
3. **7–15 seconds is the sweet spot** for music clips. Rewatches are weighted heavily, and a 10-second clip gets rewatched; a 40-second one doesn't.
4. **Loop it.** If the last frame flows into the first, the viewer watches twice without deciding to. That doubles your watch time metric for free. Design for this.
5. **Rougher is better.** A clip that looks like it was made in three minutes outperforms a clip that looks like it cost money. Polish reads as an ad, and ads get scrolled.
6. **Never post with a watermark.** TikTok demotes content with a visible Reels or CapCut watermark. Export clean from the source, always.
7. **Caption is short.** 1–2 lines, then hashtags. Long captions are an Instagram habit.
8. **Reply to comments with video.** It's a free post, it inherits the parent video's audience, and it performs disproportionately well.
9. **Post the same asset again three weeks later** with a different hook. Nobody notices, and the second one often does better.

## 8. TIKTOK SEO — THE PART EVERYONE SKIPS

**TikTok search is a real search engine and a large share of users treat it as one.** This is the biggest structural difference from Instagram and the easiest advantage to take, because most musicians post as if search doesn't exist.

**What gets indexed:**
1. **Spoken words** — auto-transcribed. Say your keywords out loud.
2. **On-screen text** — OCR'd.
3. **The caption.**
4. **Hashtags.**
5. **The sound name.**
6. **Your username and name field.**

**Your target search phrases** — say and write these, naturally, often:

`indie music india` · `indian indie artist` · `independent artist india` · `english song indian artist` · `south asian indie` · `desi indie music` · `bedroom pop india` · `indian singer songwriter` · `new indie artist 2026` · `underrated indie songs` · `songs like [comparison artist]` · `sad indie songs` · `how to release a song independently` · `music royalties explained` · `home studio india`

**The trick that works:** the last five in that list are *informational* searches, not music searches. People search them with intent. A video that answers "how to release a song independently" and happens to contain your song will be found for months, long after the algorithmic push is over. Series 1 and Series 3 exist partly for this reason.

**Concretely:** in every talking-head video, say the phrase out loud within the first 5 seconds. "I'm an independent artist in India and here's what releasing a song actually costs" contains four indexed phrases and sounds like a normal sentence.

## 10. CADENCE, TIMES AND GAPS

### 10.1 The timezone problem

Your audience is in the US, UK, Canada and UAE. **Their prime time is the middle of your night.** Do not post live.

| Target market | Their prime | Your time (IST) |
|---|---|---|
| **US East** | 7–10 PM EDT | **4:30 – 7:30 AM** |
| **US West** | 7–10 PM PDT | **7:30 – 10:30 AM** |
| **UK** | 6–9 PM BST | **10:30 PM – 1:30 AM** |
| **UAE** | 8–11 PM GST | **9:30 PM – 12:30 AM** |
| **Canada (Toronto/Brampton)** | 7–10 PM EDT | **4:30 – 7:30 AM** |

**The practical answer:** batch-schedule. One VPN session per week, 10 days of posts queued, targeting **6:00 AM IST** (US East + Canada evening) and **11:00 PM IST** (UK + UAE evening). Those two slots cover your entire target audience and you never have to be awake for either.

A useful secondary effect: TikTok posts have a long tail — a post can resurface for weeks — so slot precision matters less here than on Instagram. Get it roughly right and move on.

## 11. THE THINGS ALMOST NOBODY DOES

**Comments are a content surface.** Reply-with-video to 2–3 comments per week. These inherit the parent video's audience, take 40 seconds to make, and routinely outperform the original. Series 7 is built entirely from this.

**Pin the best comment**, not the nicest one. Pin the one that starts an argument or asks the question you want to answer.

**Pin a link comment on every video** until you have link-in-bio.

**Pin 3 videos** to your profile: (1) the best-performing one, (2) Series 1 Part 1, (3) the release video. Re-pin monthly.

**Duet and stitch other people.** Find creators asking for music recommendations, stitch with your song. Find small artists, duet their covers. This is direct, free, targeted distribution and it takes ten minutes a day.

**Repost your own old posts.** A post from six weeks ago, re-uploaded with a new hook and a new caption, is a new post. TikTok's audience turnover means nobody notices. Do this with your top 5 performers, once.

**Spark Ads, later.** If any organic post outperforms — genuinely outperforms, 10× your median — you can put ad spend behind that exact organic post rather than a made-for-ads creative. It keeps the comments and the social proof, and it converts far better than a cold ad. Only do this if something actually takes off; don't plan for it.
