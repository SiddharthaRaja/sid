## 2. THE REDUNDANCY MATRIX

> *"You go through five different platforms of mine and you find the same content everywhere. I feel like that's highly redundant."*

You were right, and it's the correct instinct — it's also the thing algorithms punish (duplicate detection, watermark demotion) and followers punish (they follow you in two places and unfollow one).

**The fix isn't posting less. It's that every platform owns something outright.**

### The eight exclusives, in one line each

1. **Instagram** — photographs, carousels, stories, highlights. The visual record.
2. **TikTok** — the numbered series, and "I can't use this app in my own country".
3. **YouTube** — the music video, the commentary, the retrospective. The permanent assets.
4. **X** — the diary, and the industry.
5. **Threads** — the questions, the craft notebook, the off-cuts.
6. **Facebook** — the ads, the groups, the Event, the older diaspora.
7. **Bluesky** — custom feeds and starter packs. Otherwise a mirror.
8. **Spotify** — Canvas, Artist Pick, the influences playlist.

### The three tests before any post

1. **Could this post exist on two of my platforms unchanged?** → Then it belongs on neither until you've rewritten it for one.
2. **Would someone who follows me in three places see this three times today?** → Stagger it or cut it.
3. **Is this platform's exclusive thing represented this week?** → If not, that's the gap to fill.

## 3. ONE ASSET, MANY SURFACES — THE ROUTING RULES

You make **one** 12-second hook clip. Here's exactly what happens to it.

| Surface | What changes | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reel** | Caption from doc 1 §7 · 3–8 tags from one hashtag set · **Slot A (9pm IST)** | 2 min |
| **TikTok** | **Different hook text in frame one** · different caption · 3–5 tags · framed as part of a series · **scheduled 6am or 11pm IST** | 2 min |
| **YouTube Short** | **Title written as a search phrase** · `#Shorts` + 2 tags · linked to the music video | 1 min |
| **Facebook Reel** | Nothing — auto-crossposts from Instagram | 0 |
| **Instagram Story** | Relay the Reel + link sticker | 10 sec |
| **X** | ❌ **No.** X gets the diary, not the clips. | — |
| **Threads** | ❌ **No.** Unless it's an off-cut, in which case it's a different clip. | — |
| **Bluesky** | ❌ No | — |

**Total: about 5 minutes per asset, three real posts plus two free ones.**

**The rules that make this work:**
- **Export clean from the source.** Never a file with a Reels or CapCut watermark — TikTok and YouTube both demote those.
- **Always change the hook text and the caption.** Same file, different object.
- **Never two assets from the same source footage back to back** on any platform. MV clip → journal video → cover → BTS → MV clip.

## 6. ⚠️ THREE CHANGES TO THE MASTER PLAN

Found while building these ten docs. All three are free — they cost nothing but a calendar edit.

**1. Move the DistroKid upload from T-28 to T-38, and the Spotify editorial pitch from T-25 to T-35.**
Spotify's current guidance and every up-to-date pitching guide recommends **4–6 weeks** of lead time, not the 7-day minimum. The editorial meetings that decide your release week happen before your release week. The old dates aren't wrong, they're just tight for no reason. *(Doc 8 §3.1.)*

**2. TikTok's density curve peaks after release, not during it.**
TikTok is blocked in India, so it contributes nothing to your domestic phase. Its peak should sit at **T+21 → T+41**, when the campaign turns toward the diaspora — not at T like every other platform. *(Doc 2 §0.)*

**3. The "no filming beyond the music video" constraint holds — but only because most of the bank isn't filming.**
164 assets are available without a second shoot day, against 151 needed. What makes that true is that ~45 are journal-as-video, ~18 are design, ~24 are phone-at-desk captures and ~12 are screen recordings. **The single highest-yield free decision left is asking someone to film BTS on a phone at the video shoot.** *(Doc 1 §2.)*

## 8. THE WEEKLY OPERATING RHYTHM

You asked for posting to require **"zero energy, zero brain."** That's achievable, but only if the thinking happens once a week rather than every day. This is the system.

### ⭐ SUNDAY — THE BATCH HOUR (90 minutes, the only day you think)

1. **Review** (10 min) — last week's top 2 and bottom 2 Reels. Make two more like the top 2 next week. Never make another like the bottom 2.
2. **Pull 10–14 assets** from the bank for the coming week. Check the alternation rule: never two from the same source consecutively.
3. **Write the captions and hooks** for all of them, in one sitting. Shop from doc 1 §7, doc 2 §6, doc 3 §4.3.
4. **Schedule Instagram + Facebook** in Meta Business Suite for the week.
5. **Schedule YouTube Shorts** for the week.
6. **Write the week's 7 diary entries** *(if you're not already ahead — and you should be ahead)*.
7. **Queue the week's Threads posts** in a notes file.

### WEDNESDAY — THE VPN SESSION (20 minutes)
Batch-schedule **10 days** of TikTok in one go. Once a week, and you never touch the VPN again until next Wednesday.

### EVERY DAY — THE 25 MINUTES
| | |
|---|---|
| Post the 3 story frames | 1 min |
| Post the diary entry at 10:30pm, mirror to Bluesky at 1am | 2 min |
| Reply to every comment and DM from the day | 10 min |
| 10 replies on X, zero about yourself | 7 min |
| 5 replies on Threads | 5 min |

### MONDAY — THE NUMBERS (10 minutes, once a week, not daily)
Check: median views per Reel · % non-followers · Spotify saves and save rate · smart-link clicks by source · ad cost per conversion. **Kill what's above your target cost, double what's below.** Then close the tabs until next Monday.

### THE HARD GATE
> **From `rollout-density-analysis.md`: anything not shot or written before T-10 will not get made.** Post-release capacity goes entirely to posting, replying and chasing curators. Treat "the content bank is built" as a hard prerequisite for picking the release date — exactly like the DistroKid deadline.

## 9. THE DASHBOARD

Eight numbers. Nothing else, for the first 60 days.

| # | Metric | Where | Healthy | If it's bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Median views per Reel** | IG Insights | rising week over week | The only number that matters in month one. **Not follower count.** |
| 2 | **% non-followers** | Per Reel | >70% | Below 50% = not reaching a cold pool. Change the hook. |
| 3 | **Sends per reach** | Per Reel | >1% | Instagram's most-weighted signal. Make things worth sending. |
| 4 | **Spotify save rate** | Spotify for Artists | >8% is decent for a debut | Ask for saves by name, in those words, everywhere |
| 5 | **Completion rate** | Spotify for Artists | — | If people skip in the first 30s, the intro is too long |
| 6 | **Smart-link clicks by source** | Linkfire | — | Tells you which platform actually converts. **Reallocate accordingly.** |
| 7 | **Ad cost per conversion** | Meta Ads | — | Review every 4 days. Kill losers fast. |
| 8 | **Playlist adds** | Spotify for Artists | — | Each add = a warm lead for that curator's *other* playlists |

**Two traps.** Don't check daily in month one — the numbers will be small and the cost is morale, not information. And **track median views per post, not follower count**: climbing views at 40 followers means it's working and followers are simply lagging.

## 10. THE FIVE FAILURE MODES

From the density analysis, plus two more these documents surfaced.

| # | Failure | What it looks like | The counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The month-one void** | ~50 posts to almost nobody before anything moves | Track median views, not followers. Expect it. It's not a signal. |
| **2** | **Nothing gets shot after T-10** | The bank you have at T-10 is the bank you'll ever have | Gate the release date on the bank being built |
| **3** | **⭐ Day 40** | Where independent campaigns die — after release-week adrenaline, before traction | Schedule two weeks ahead, always. The T+42 acoustic release exists specifically to give day 40 an event. |
| **4** | **Looking like a campaign, not a person** | Five platforms, one voice, everything is an advert | The redundancy matrix (§2). Never two assets from the same source in a row. The diary never sells. |
| **5** | **The journal running dry** | 80 entries to go and it can't be batched | Write 3 a night. **It's the only unbatchable item in the entire plan and it's the critical path.** |
| **6** | **The double-follower burn** *(new)* | Someone follows you on IG and Threads and sees the same thing twice | Auto-share IG→Threads **off**. §2. |
| **7** | **Ask fatigue** *(new)* | A link in every post, and people stop seeing you | Max one ask per 48 hours per platform. Roughly 1 caption in 3, never more. |

## 11. IF YOU ONLY DO TEN THINGS

Everything above is the complete version. This is the version that still works.

1. **Ask someone to film BTS on a phone all day at the video shoot.** Free. Yields ~15 assets in a visual world that isn't the polished video.
2. **Write 3 diary entries a night until you have 100.** The critical path.
3. **Post 1–3 clips a day to Reels + TikTok + Shorts.** Different hook each time.
4. **Upload to DistroKid at T-38. Pitch Spotify editorial at T-35.** The only two hard deadlines.
5. **Message your 40 closest people individually at T-7.** Not a broadcast.
6. **Ask for *saves* and *playlist adds* by name.** Those exact words.
7. **Send 80–120 personalised curator emails from T-21.** One playlist per email.
8. **Repost every single share on release day.**
9. **Release the acoustic version at T+42.** A second event for the cost of an afternoon.
10. **Write the post-mortem at T+55.** It's what makes release #2 twice as effective — and release #2 inside 12 weeks is the only thing that makes any of this compound.
