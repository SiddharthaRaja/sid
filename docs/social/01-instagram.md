## 1. THE THESIS

**Instagram is your reach engine and your home.** Everything else in this campaign is either a feeder into it or a different audience entirely.

Three jobs, in priority order:

1. **Reels do discovery.** This is where a stranger meets the song. Every Reel is scored independently against a cold pool — no follower fatigue, no volume penalty. More Reels is strictly better up to the limit of your asset bank. This is ~70% of the value of the platform.
2. **Feed does credibility.** When a stranger from a Reel taps your profile, the grid is the two-second decision: *is this a real artist or a person posting once?* The grid doesn't need reach. It needs to look inhabited.
3. **Stories do depth.** Stories reach people who already follow you. They convert a follower into someone who will actually press play on release day. Stories are where the *asking* happens — the link sticker, the countdown, the "here's how to help."

**What Instagram is not for:** it is not where you meet curators (that's X), not where the streams get counted (that's the smart link), and not where your writing lives (that's X/Threads). Don't try to make it do those jobs.

## 2. THE CONSTRAINT RECALIBRATION ⚠️

You've said: assume a high probability you film **nothing beyond the music video**.

Taken literally that breaks the plan — the density analysis needs ~151 short-form assets and the MV yields ~35. So let's be precise about what the constraint actually is, because I think it's narrower than it sounds.

**A *production* is a shoot.** Crew, location, permission, a day off, other people's schedules. The music video is a production. The "Vertical Day" and "Room Day" in the density analysis were framed as productions, and it's completely reasonable to say you'll only do one.

**A *capture* is not a shoot.** A phone on a stack of books, pointed at you at your desk, for eleven minutes, while you say four things into it. No crew, no location, no permission, no day off. You can do a capture at 1am in a t-shirt you've worn for two days.

Almost everything in the banks below is a capture, a design, or a screen recording. Here is the honest arithmetic:

| Source | Assets | Requires a shoot? |
|---|---|---|
| Music video clips (hook ×10, verse/bridge ×8, b-roll loops ×8, crop/speed/caption variants ×9) | **35** | Already happening |
| Journal-as-video (text on screen, b-roll or still underneath, song under it) | **45** | No — `[DESIGN]` |
| Lyric cards, typography, animated stills | **18** | No — `[DESIGN]` |
| Talking-to-camera captures (phone, desk, 4–6 per sitting) | **24** | No — `[PHONE]` |
| DAW / screen recordings — production breakdowns, sessions, analytics | **12** | No — `[SCREEN]` |
| Covers and fragments, phone at a desk with a guitar | **10** | No — `[PHONE]` |
| Reaction / milestone / data | **10** | No — `[DESIGN]`/`[SCREEN]` |
| Fan content, duets, reposts | **10** | No — `[FOUND]` |
| **Total available without a second shoot day** | **164** | |

**164 against 151 needed. The constraint holds.** You do not need a second production. You do need roughly **six evenings** across the pre-release window: two for talking-head captures, two for journal-video assembly, one for screen recordings, one for covers.

**But the constraint has a real cost, and you should know what it is:**

- **Visual monotony.** Every performance frame comes from one location, one outfit, one light. A follower who sees forty Reels sees the same room forty times. The journal videos and design assets are what save you — they don't look like the MV. **Rule: never post two MV-sourced clips in a row.** Alternate MV → non-MV → MV → non-MV, permanently.
- **No B-plot.** The MV gives you one visual world. Great campaigns have two or three. Your second world will have to be *your actual desk*, shot honestly — that's the "person" pillar, and it's cheap.
- **One thing to reconsider:** the highest-yield hour in the entire campaign is asking whoever is on the MV shoot to film **BTS on a phone all day**. It costs you nothing, needs no extra day, and yields ~15 assets in a visual world that isn't the polished video. If you do one thing from this document, do that.

**The one genuinely unrecoverable asset:** the "stills day". Photographs are what feed the *grid*, and the grid is 104 posts of this plan. Without a photographer you'll pull stills from MV frames — which works, but every grid photo will also be one location. Mitigation: 15 minutes with a friend and a phone, in daylight, against a plain wall, in two shirts. That's not a shoot. That's a coffee break, and it yields 40 usable frames.

## 3. ACCOUNT ARCHITECTURE

Build this once, at T-45. It then runs itself.

### 3.1 The profile

| Field | What to put | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Username** | `[HANDLE]` — identical to every other platform | Searchability, and people will type it from a TikTok |
| **Name field** | `[ARTIST] · [GENRE]` | ⚠️ **This field is indexed by Instagram search. Your username is not, semantically.** Putting your genre here is the single highest-value SEO move on the platform, and it takes four seconds. |
| **Account type** | Professional → **Creator** → Category **Musician/Band** | Creator (not Business) keeps full access to the commercial music library, which you need for using other people's audio |
| **Profile photo** | 1000×1000, your face filling ~60% of the frame, legible at 40px | It renders at 40px in a comment thread. A wide shot reads as a grey smudge. |
| **Bio** | Rotates by phase — see 3.2 | |
| **Link** | `[LINK]` — always the smart link, never a raw Spotify URL | UTM attribution is the whole reason the smart link exists |
| **Contact button** | Artist email | Curators and small blogs do use it |
| **Profile song** | Set to `[SONG]` at T | Free permanent placement on your own profile |

### 3.5 Broadcast Channel

Create it at T-30. Name it something that isn't "[ARTIST] updates" — name it like a group chat: `the back room`, `[ARTIST] — notes`, `songs and complaints`.

This is **owned audience**. The feed algorithm cannot take it away from you. Every person in it gets a push notification when you post. Treat it accordingly:

- Invite via story, 3 times across the campaign, never more
- Post 2–3× per week, not daily
- Content that goes **only** here: unfinished voice memos, the demo, the lyric you cut, the release-day live count, "should I release the acoustic version"
- Use polls in it — it's the highest-response-rate surface you own
- **At T-7, the channel gets the ask before anyone else.** They're your first 48 hours.

### 3.6 Close Friends

Build to ~50 people by T-20. Real friends, people who've replied to stories, the street team.

Close Friends stories get seen at a dramatically higher rate than public stories, and being on the list makes people feel like insiders — which is what makes them repost without being asked. Use it for: the first listen, the cover art vote, the "I'm nervous" post the night before, and the release-day ask.

### 3.7 Things to switch on once

- [ ] Reels **remix** enabled, **downloads** enabled — you want people taking your clips
- [ ] **Original audio** named properly (it defaults to your username; rename it to `[SONG] — [ARTIST]` where possible)
- [ ] Comment filters off (don't hide replies; engagement is engagement)
- [ ] Linked to the **Facebook Page** in Meta Business Suite — required before you can run a single ad
- [ ] **Auto-crosspost Reels to Facebook: ON.** Free surface, zero work. (Crossposting to Threads: **off** — Threads gets its own thing, see doc 5.)
- [ ] **Notes** (the little text bubble at the top of DMs) — a free, recurring, 60-character text surface almost nobody uses. Change it 2× a week. It's the cheapest impression on the platform.

## 4. REELS — 154 IDEAS

**Reels are ~70% of the value of Instagram for you.** Post 1–3 per day depending on phase (see §9).

### The rules that apply to every single one

1. **The hook is audible in the first 1.5 seconds.** No build-up, no "wait for it", no 3-second logo. If the song is in the clip, the song starts at frame one.
2. **Burned-in captions, always.** ~80% of short-form is watched muted. A music clip watched muted is a wasted post unless the lyric is on screen.
3. **7–20 seconds.** Under 7 and it won't hold; over 20 and completion rate falls off a cliff. The single best length for a music clip is **9–13 seconds.**
4. **On-screen text in the first frame** — Instagram OCRs it and it's a ranking input. It's also the thing that stops the scroll.
5. **Never two MV-sourced clips in a row.** (See §2.)
6. **Every Reel also goes to TikTok and YouTube Shorts the same day.** One asset, three posts. See doc 10.
7. **Reuse aggressively.** The same hook clip with a different caption and different on-screen text is a *different post* to the algorithm. The bank below has ~154 ideas, but the honest number of distinct *assets* needed is ~90, because roughly 60 of these are re-cuts.

## 5. STORIES — 137 PROMPTS + 9 SCRIPTED SEQUENCES

### 5.1 How to think about stories

Stories are **not content.** Stories are a relay and a conversation. They reach people who already follow you, and their job is to turn a follower into someone who will actually press play on Friday.

**The 3-frame daily system.** Every day, forever, three frames. Only one of them requires any thought.

| Frame | What | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The relay** | Today's Reel, pushed to story, with the link sticker on it | 10 seconds |
| **2. The human one** | A phone photo of wherever you are + one sentence | 15 seconds |
| **3. The interactive one** | A poll, question box, slider, quiz or countdown | 20 seconds |

That's 45 seconds a day and it's 80% of the value. Everything below is the other 20%.

**Rules:**
- **Link sticker at least once a week, forever.** More often in release week. The most common failure in an independent campaign is going three weeks without telling anyone where to listen.
- **Reply to every single story reply.** A story reply is a DM. A DM is the strongest relationship signal Instagram has, and a person who has DM'd you will almost always press play.
- **Repost every single share.** During release week this is the highest-ROI activity of the entire day — it makes the next person share, because they can see they'll be seen.
- **Don't post 14 frames a day.** People tap through. 3–6 is the band; release day is the one exception.

### 5.2 The interactive sticker playbook

Every response is an engagement signal, and each sticker type does a different job.

| Sticker | Job | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Poll (2 option)** | Highest response rate of anything. People tap without thinking. | Mix A/B, cover art, which lyric, verse or chorus, "should I post the acoustic" |
| **Question box** | Depth. Gives you content for a week. | "ask me anything about the song", "what should I cover", "what's a song you love with under 100k streams" |
| **Quiz** | Playful, and it teaches. | "which lyric is real", "guess the instrument", "what's this song about" |
| **Slider** | Low-commitment, feels like a vote | "how nervous should I be", "rate the cover art" |
| **Countdown** | Sets a notification the viewer opts into. **The single best pre-release sticker.** | Announcement day, release day, video premiere, acoustic drop |
| **Link** | The only one that makes money | `[LINK]`, every week |
| **Add Yours** | Turns your story into a chain across other people's accounts | "your song of the year", "first concert" — put yours in the prompt |
| **Music** | Free placement of your own track | Use your own song on other people's reposts |
| **Reveal** | Pay-with-a-message to unlock | The demo, the unreleased verse, the cover art |

## 6. FEED — 94 IDEAS

### 6.1 What the feed is for

**The grid is the decision surface.** A stranger arrives from a Reel, taps your name, and gives you roughly two seconds. They're not reading — they're pattern-matching for *is this a real artist*. That's it. That's the whole job.

Which means:

- The grid does not need reach. Stop optimising feed posts for reach.
- The grid needs to be **full, coherent and current.** 9 posts is a profile. 3 is a trial account.
- **Carousels outperform single images on saves and shares by a wide margin**, and saves are the strongest ranking signal on the platform. Default to carousels.
- **Post 2× per week.** More than 3 and you're eating your own story audience for no gain.
- **The first slide of a carousel is a cover, not content.** Give it a title in text.

### 6.6 Feed hygiene

- **Alt text on every post.** Two seconds, and it's directly indexed by search. Write it as a sentence with your keywords: *"[ARTIST], an independent [GENRE] artist from [CITY], singing into a microphone in a home studio."*

## 7. CAPTIONS — 126 WRITTEN + THE FORMULAS

### 7.1 The four rules

1. **The first line is the whole caption.** Instagram truncates at ~125 characters. Everything after "…more" is read by maybe 15% of people. Put the hook, the joke, or the ask in the first line — never the setup.
2. **Lowercase reads as a person. Title Case reads as a brand.** You are a person. Use lowercase almost everywhere, and break it deliberately when something matters.
3. **One ask per caption, maximum, and not in every caption.** A CTA in every single post trains people to scroll past. Aim for a CTA in roughly 1 in 3.
4. **A caption of zero words is a valid caption** and reads as confidence. Use it about once a month, on your best-looking asset.

## 8. SEO, KEYWORDS AND HASHTAGS

### 8.1 The honest hierarchy

Instagram's discovery in 2026 is **keyword and semantic**, not hashtag-driven. Hashtag *following* was removed, and Instagram's own leadership has repeatedly said hashtags do not meaningfully drive reach. What actually ranks you:

| Rank | Signal | Effort | Most people ignore it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Watch time and completion rate** on the Reel | The clip itself | — |
| 2 | **Sends per reach** (DM shares) — Instagram's single most weighted signal | Make things worth sending | Yes |
| 3 | **The Name field** on your profile | 4 seconds, once | **Yes** |
| 4 | **Keywords in the first line of the caption** | Free | **Yes** |
| 5 | **On-screen text** (OCR'd) and **spoken words** (transcribed) | Free | **Yes** |
| 6 | **Alt text** | 2 seconds per post | **Yes** |
| 7 | Saves | — | — |
| 8 | **Hashtags** | Free | No — everyone over-does these |

**Conclusion: use 3–8 hashtags, not 30.** A wall of 30 tags is a mild spam signal and does approximately nothing. The sets below exist so you can pick 3–8 *relevant* ones quickly — not so you can paste all of them.

### 8.2 The keyword layer — do this before you think about hashtags

**Your keyword set.** Pick 8–10 phrases a real person would type into Instagram search to find music like yours. For you, likely:

`indian indie artist` · `indie music india` · `independent artist india` · `[GENRE] india` · `bedroom pop india` · `singer songwriter india` · `english song indian artist` · `desi indie` · `south asian indie` · `[CITY] musician`

Then put them where they're indexed:

- **Name field:** `[ARTIST] · [GENRE]` — do this today
- **First line of caption:** work one keyword in naturally, roughly every third post
- **On-screen text:** the first frame of a Reel is OCR'd
- **Spoken audio:** say your genre out loud in talking-head clips — the transcription is indexed
- **Alt text:** one descriptive sentence per feed post, with a keyword in it
- **Your username:** keep it the same everywhere so cross-platform search resolves to you

### 8.3 The 18 hashtag sets

Pick **one set per post**, then take **3–8 tags from it**. Rotate sets — don't use the same block every time, that's the pattern Instagram flags.

**Mix rule:** for each post, aim for roughly 1 huge tag (>1M), 3 mid tags (50k–500k), 2–3 small/niche tags (<50k). The small ones are where you actually get found; the big ones are context.

### 8.4 Hashtag hygiene

- **Put them in the caption, not the first comment.** The first-comment trick hasn't mattered for years and it costs you the keyword indexing.
- **Check before you use a niche tag.** Some tags get quietly restricted and a restricted tag can suppress the whole post. Search the tag first — if the page shows a warning or shows nothing, don't use it.
- **Never use tags unrelated to the post.** `#viral #fyp #explorepage #foryou` do nothing on Instagram and are a spam signal here (they're a TikTok habit).
- **Don't reuse the identical block twice in a row.** Vary at least 3 tags between consecutive posts.
- **Branded tag:** create one — `#[SONG]` or `#[ARTIST]` — and put it on everything, so fan content collects somewhere findable.

## 9. CADENCE, TIME GAPS, AND THE CALENDAR

### 9.1 The posting clock

You are targeting India first, then diaspora, then the West. These four slots cover that, in IST.

| Slot | IST | India | UAE | UK | US East | US West | Australia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — the all-rounder** | **9:00–9:30 PM** | prime | 7:30 PM prime | 4:30 PM | 11:30 AM | 8:30 AM | ✗ |
| **B — the diaspora slot** | **8:00–8:30 AM** | morning | 6:30 AM | ✗ | **10:30 PM prime** | **7:30 PM prime** | 12:30 PM |
| **C — the India slot** | **1:00–1:30 PM** | lunch | 11:30 AM | 8:30 AM | ✗ | ✗ | 5:30 PM |
| **D — the late slot** | **11:00 PM–12:00 AM** | late scroll | 9:30 PM | 6:30 PM | 1:30 PM | 10:30 AM | ✗ |

**How to use them:**
- **1 post/day** → Slot A. Always.
- **2 posts/day** → Slot C + Slot A (India phase) · Slot A + Slot B (diaspora phase)
- **3 posts/day** → Slot B + Slot C + Slot A
- **Stories** → spread across the day, don't batch them at midnight
- **Feed posts** → Slot A or D, and never within 3 hours of a Reel

### 9.2 The time gaps — the actual numbers

| Gap between | Minimum | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Two Reels | **4 hours** (6+ is better) | Posted closer together they compete for the same delivery slot and the second one gets throttled |
| A Reel and a feed post | **3 hours** | Same reason, milder |
| Two feed posts | **48 hours** | Feed posts eat your follower attention; you have a fixed budget of it |
| Two story sessions | **3 hours** | Back-to-back frames get tapped through as one block |
| Two *asks* (link, pre-save, "please share") | **48 hours** | The fastest way to train people to scroll past you |
| Two milestone posts | **7 days** | Milestones only feel like milestones if they're rare |
| Two posts from the same source asset | **1 post** — i.e. never consecutive | The monotony problem from §2 |
| A personal DM ask and the next one | **21 days** | You get roughly two of these per release. Spend them at T-7 and T+30. |
| Announcement → release | **28–30 days** | Less and pre-saves don't accumulate; more and the announcement goes stale |
| Content spikes (a "real" event) | **every 12–16 days** | Release, lyric video, acoustic drop, milestone. Without one, week 4 onward flatlines. |

### 9.5 What to actually watch

Ignore follower count for the first 30 days. It lags everything and it will make you miserable.

| Metric | Where | What good looks like | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Median views per Reel** | Insights → Reels | Rising, week over week | This is the only number that matters in month one |
| **% non-followers** | Per Reel | >70% | Below 50% = it's not reaching a cold pool; change the hook |
| **Watch time / completion** | Per Reel | >90% for a 10s clip | Below 70% = the clip is too long or the first second is wrong |
| **Sends per reach** | Per Reel | >1% is strong | Instagram's most weighted signal. Make things worth sending. |
| **Saves** | Per post | Carousels should beat Reels here | Low saves on a carousel = it wasn't useful |
| **Story completion rate** | Per sequence | >60% through a 5-frame sequence | Big drop at frame 2 = frame 1 was a promise you didn't keep |
| **Link sticker taps** | Story insights | — | The only story metric connected to streams |
| **Profile visits → follows** | Insights | >15% | Low = the grid isn't doing its job. Fix the grid, not the Reels. |

**The kill/double rule:** every Sunday, look at the week's Reels. Find the top 2 and the bottom 2. Make two more like the top 2 next week. Never make another like the bottom 2. Do this for eight weeks and the account will have taught you what works better than any guide can.
